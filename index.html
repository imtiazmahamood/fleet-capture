What each tab is for

Daily LogDrivers log km at the end of each day. Start km fills in automatically from the last closing km recorded for that vehicle - just enter today's closing odometer reading. Fuel fill-ups and repair reports can be added from the same screen.
Vehicle MasterAdd and edit the fleet's master data - one place to keep reg number, make/model, fuel type, assigned driver, station, status, opening odometer, and insurance/licence expiry dates current.
MaintenanceEvery service, repair, tyre change, or upgrade gets logged here with workshop, cost, invoice reference and status, whether entered directly or reported by a driver from the Daily Log.
Tyres & InspectionOne row per vehicle tracking tyre size/brand per position, replacement dates, inspection dates and roadworthy status.
Driver AccountabilityRecord incidents, fines, damage or commendations against a driver and vehicle, with fault and resolution status.
DashboardLive totals, expiry/service alerts, spend charts, the approval queue for daily log entries, and auto-calculated monthly and per-vehicle cost summaries - no manual totalling required.
